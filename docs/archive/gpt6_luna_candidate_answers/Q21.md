## Answer

There isn’t a stated acceptable leak *rate* in the material provided. The practical standard is that the tank should hold the plans’ test pressure with **no measurable pressure loss over several days**, once temperature and barometric-pressure changes are accounted for.

A drop of about **¼ inch of water per hour** would be evidence of a leak, not an acceptable rate. Small changes between readings can come from temperature, weather, or the test setup, so check the fittings and repeat the test under steady conditions. Use the plans’ specified test pressure and a manometer arranged to relieve excess pressure—don’t pressurize the tank beyond the recommended limit.
