## Answer

The context does **not** establish a single approved grease for the nose-gear fork. For the **shimmy-damper friction surfaces** (the plates and shaft), ordinary wheel-bearing grease is not clearly recommended: reports say thicker greases can get squeezed out and reduce the damper’s preload. One builder suggested washing-machine damping grease, but that is only a suggestion, not a confirmed specification.

Keep the damper adjusted to the specified friction—about **2–4 lb** to pivot the fork—and follow the aircraft plans or component maker’s instructions for your specific assembly. Don’t confuse the damper surfaces with the nose-wheel bearings, which need their own appropriate bearing grease.
