## Answer

For the **plans-based Cozy fuel lines**, use **3/8-inch OD aluminum tubing with a 0.035-inch wall, type 3003**. The **vent lines are 1/4-inch tubing**.

Use appropriately rated fuel hose where a flexible connection is needed, and follow the plans and engine manufacturer’s specifications for any nonstandard fuel system.
