## Answer

There isn’t enough comparative Cozy data to say one location is universally best. **Good, sealed airflow through the cooler matters more than its position.**

- **Plans location—top of the firewall:** A sensible default if it fits. It can provide airflow over the accessory case, including after parking, but requires a properly designed inlet and upper-cowl exit.
- **Lower cowl behind the strake:** Also a proven option. One reported 13-row installation kept oil below 180°F in climb, but its ducting and exit were carefully arranged. Don’t assume a cooler will work well just because it sits in the lower cowl.
- **Under the engine:** Some builders report success, but consider vibration and mount strength, and make sure the inlet-to-exit airflow is adequate.

For cooler choices, the context mentions a **13-row Aero Classic** installation that worked well, and a **13-row Stewart-Warner** as a suggested choice for an approximately 185-hp installation. An **Aero Classic 17-row** has also been used, but may be more cooler than necessary for some setups; sizing depends on engine, climate, and actual oil temperatures. I wouldn’t choose the largest cooler automatically.

**Practical recommendation:** Use the plans’ top-firewall location if it packages well; otherwise, a well-ducted lower-cowl/strake installation is a reasonable alternative. Match the cooler to your engine and verify temperatures in flight. The best specific choice depends on your engine model and cooling-air arrangement.
