## Answer

There isn’t enough evidence here to say which is **most common overall**—the discussions are anecdotes, not a survey. The **plans trim** is likely common among builders because it is the original, straightforward option and some choose it to finish the aircraft sooner.

Among the three, **Davenport is often the preference of people choosing or replacing a system**. Owners praise its simplicity and lack of hysteresis, and one experienced builder said they’d choose it if starting from scratch. But it must be carefully sized, tuned, and inspected; a poorly designed or damaged spring can fail.

The **Strong trim** also has satisfied long-term users, but reports mention sensitivity, hysteresis, maintenance issues, and limited availability.

**Practical takeaway:** Davenport seems to get the strongest recommendations, but none is automatically safest or best. Whichever you use, verify full control authority with full opposite trim, and inspect the spring and attachments closely.
