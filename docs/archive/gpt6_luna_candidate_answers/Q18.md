## Answer

F28 was moved aft when builders discovered the canard’s hotwire template was about 0.3 inches longer than the cross-section shown on the M drawing. If F28 had been installed at the plans’ 5.9-inch position, the canard could interfere with it, so the suggested fix was to relocate F28 to 6.25 inches when fitting the canard. The change was documented in the builders’ FAQ and discussed by 2010.
