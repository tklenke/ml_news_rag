## Answer

For the **Saber extension-to-crankshaft/flange bolts**, the cited Saber torque ranges are:

- **3/8 in.: 30–35 ft-lb**
- **7/16 in.: 35–45 ft-lb**
- **1/2 in.: 40–50 ft-lb**

If you mean the **propeller bolts attaching the prop to the extension**, use the prop manufacturer’s specification instead; that torque can differ. Verify the bolt size and current Saber/aircraft instructions, and use a calibrated torque wrench.
