## Answer

Owners seem **cautious but not categorically opposed** to alternative engines such as Rotax. The conventional Lycoming is viewed as the lower-risk choice because its Cozy installation is well understood; an alternative can work, but it means more engineering and testing—especially for cooling, weight and balance, and propeller compatibility.

Rotax’s lighter weight and strong high-altitude performance appeal to some builders, and owners point to successful non-Lycoming installations as proof that alternatives are viable. But the Rotax’s cost and the limited experience with it in Cozys make some owners question whether the benefits justify the work. Overall, the attitude is: **open to alternatives, provided the builder does the homework, but generally more confident in a proven installation.**
