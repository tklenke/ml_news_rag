## Answer

If **only EZ 87B is discontinued**, the closest substitute is **EZ 10A resin with EZ 83B hardener**; **EZ 92B** is also in the same group. The technical guidance in the material you provided says 83B, 87B and 92B have essentially the same fuel resistance, with differences mainly in pot life and mixed viscosity. **EZ 84B is not the equivalent choice for gasoline with ethanol**; it was reported to be less resistant than that group.

If EZ 10A itself is unavailable, the sources don’t establish a proven, drop-in replacement with the same long-term track record. Ampreg 21 has encouraging reported experience—including a long-term E10-soaked coupon—but that is not the same as demonstrating equivalence. Other laminating epoxies or an interior fuel-tank coating should be evaluated separately; a coating such as a polysulfide sealant is a barrier, **not** a substitute for the structural laminating resin.

Whichever system is used, follow its cure and post-cure requirements and verify current product availability and suitability with the supplier or a qualified composite-aircraft adviser.
