## Answer

The Cozy plans specify about **2.1 inches of aileron travel up and down from neutral**, measured at the **inboard trailing edge**—roughly **20° each way**. A commonly cited plans tolerance is **±0.2 inches**; one edition of the POH gives **±0.3 inches**.
