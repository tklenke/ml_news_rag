## Answer

The goal is **no detectable leak**, rather than an accepted leak rate. In the builder discussions, a drop of **0.25 inch of water per hour** was considered indicative of a leak that should be found and repaired.

Monitor the manometer over several days, accounting for temperature and atmospheric-pressure changes; these can move the water level even in a leak-free tank. Also check the test fittings for leaks. Follow the Cozy plans for test pressure and use a manometer arranged to relieve excess pressure—overpressurizing can damage the tank.
