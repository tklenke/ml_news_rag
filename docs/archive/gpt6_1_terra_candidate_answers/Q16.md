## Answer

For a Cozy with the **Roncz canard**, set the incidence using the corrected **template F from Newsletter 80**, referenced to the longerons. The template’s level line should read level; that does **not** mean the airfoil’s actual incidence is zero.

The intended result is elevators in trail (0°) or slightly reflexed in cruise at a mid-range center of gravity. Biasing the setting toward the high side of the template’s practical tolerance can help avoid setting the canard too low.

There isn’t a single reliable degree figure in the supplied material: the canard’s incidence **relative to the wings** also matters. Have an experienced Cozy builder or qualified inspector assess any suspected mismatch before making changes.
