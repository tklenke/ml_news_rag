## Answer

Vortex generators (VGs) appear **promising for reducing Cozy landing speeds**, but the evidence in the supplied reports is mostly anecdotal.

- Owners using VGs on **both the canard and main wing** have claimed landing-speed reductions of roughly **5–10 knots**. One Cozy IV owner reported an approximately **8-knot reduction in minimum speed**, along with about an **8-knot loss in top speed**.
- A lower touchdown speed could shorten the landing roll, but the reports don’t provide a measured before-and-after **landing-distance reduction for a Cozy**. The actual saving would depend on the approach, flare, braking, runway and conditions.

There’s an important safety qualification: Marc Zeitlin noted that he had not seen documented before-and-after stall-speed tests across all configurations. A Cozy that *feels* better at low speed does not necessarily have enough stall margin to justify a slower approach. Determine the aircraft’s canard-stall behavior across its weight and CG range before changing approach speeds, and get experienced Cozy flight-test guidance for any modification.
