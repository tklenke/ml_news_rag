## Answer

For your Cozy’s **nose wheel**, look for the torque and assembly instructions in:

1. **The wheel manufacturer’s maintenance manual**, using the make and part number stamped on the wheel. This is the best source for the wheel-half bolt torque and bearing or axle-nut adjustment.
2. **Your Cozy plans and builder’s records**, including any modifications. Amateur-built aircraft may have different wheels or axle arrangements.
3. **FAA AC 43.13-1B, Chapter 7**, for general fastener torque guidance where appropriate—but don’t substitute a generic bolt value for a wheel manufacturer’s specific instructions or bearing-preload procedure.

The supplied references don’t give a nose-wheel or axle torque value. Also, **fully deflate the tire before separating the wheel halves**. If the installed parts or instructions aren’t clear, consult an aircraft mechanic before returning the aircraft to service.

Do you know the Cozy model and the wheel make and part number? Are you asking about the wheel-half bolts, the axle nut, or the axle attachment bolts?
