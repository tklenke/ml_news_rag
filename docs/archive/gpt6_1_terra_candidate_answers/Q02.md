## Answer

The Bendix RSA-5 servo doesn’t determine a single cable length for a Cozy. The length depends on your quadrant location, cable routing, servo orientation and attachment brackets. One Cozy III builder reported using a **96-inch throttle cable**; another Cozy installation used a **108-inch cable**. Treat those as reference points, not order dimensions.

### How to measure

1. **Install or mock up the servo and control brackets.** Establish where each cable housing will be secured at the cockpit and engine ends, and how its inner cable will attach to the servo lever. Make sure the cockpit controls move the throttle and mixture in the intended directions.

2. **Lay out each route separately.** Use flexible tubing, cord or a tape to trace the actual path from the cockpit attachment to the servo attachment. Allow smooth bends, clearance from hot or moving parts, and enough flexibility for engine movement. A side-of-tub route with a 45° or 90° turn toward the servo may be possible, but check the cable manufacturer’s minimum bend radius.

3. **Measure along each proposed route.** Record throttle and mixture lengths independently. Also measure the travel needed at each servo lever and compare it with the available cockpit-control travel.

4. **Confirm the ordering dimensions with the cable supplier.** “Cable length” can mean overall length, housing length or another specified dimension. Give the supplier your route measurements, required travel, end fittings and mounting details rather than assuming their nominal length matches your tape measurement.

5. **Check the installation before flight.** The controls must reach both servo stops without binding, while retaining a little cockpit-control travel at each end. Check clearance and operation throughout the engine’s permitted movement. Where practical, anchor the cable housings to the engine or servo bracket to reduce relative movement.

Use properly retained linkage hardware appropriate to the servo; don’t select a ball-socket fitting solely because it threads onto the cable. Have an experienced aircraft mechanic or qualified Cozy builder review the routing, attachments and rigging before flight.
