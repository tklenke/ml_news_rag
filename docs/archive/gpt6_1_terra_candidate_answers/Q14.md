## Answer

For the Cozy MKIV, the plans specify **2.1 ± 0.2 inches of aileron travel** up and down from neutral, measured at the inboard trailing edge.

The first-edition owner’s manual gives a slightly different tolerance: **2.1 ± 0.3 inches**. Verify the applicable specification against your aircraft’s plans and operating handbook.
