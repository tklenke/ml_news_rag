## Answer

The supplied material does **not specify a recommended grease for the Cozy nose gear fork**.

For the fork’s shimmy-damper friction discs, one owner used white grease; another reported that thicker greases squeezed out and the damper preload dropped. Graphite was also reported to perform worse. For the nose-wheel bearings, an owner proposed wheel-bearing grease, but the excerpt does not confirm a particular product.

Check the instructions for your specific fork and damper before lubricating them, and verify the damper friction after maintenance. Incorrect damper friction can allow shimmy and lead to fork failure.
