## Answer

For a Cozy Mk IV, the **plans-preferred starting point is at the top of the firewall**, exhausting through the upper cowling. It also encourages airflow past the accessory case after shutdown. If space is tight, the **lower cowling behind either strake** is a commonly successful alternative.

There isn’t enough comparative data to say one location is universally best. Providing a good pressure difference and an unobstructed path **through the cooler to its exit** matters as much as location.

### Coolers mentioned by builders

- **Stewart-Warner, 13-row:** Suggested for an approximately 185-hp installation.
- **Aero Classic, 17-row:** One builder uses it on the lower right cowl. Another described it as oversized for a Cozy, but potentially useful in hot, high-altitude conditions if the extra weight and space are acceptable.
- **Aero Classic, reportedly 13-row:** One lower-cowl installation reportedly kept oil below 180°F in climbs, though the owner was uncertain of the brand.

The supplied accounts don’t give reliable part numbers, so confirm the exact cooler dimensions and specifications before ordering. Size it for your engine and operating conditions, and verify oil temperatures in ground and flight testing against the engine manufacturer’s limits. Consult an experienced Cozy builder or mechanic on mounting, airflow, and vibration.
