## Answer

For the Cozy’s fuel lines through the strakes and fuselage, the plans call for **3003-O aluminum tubing, 3/8-inch outside diameter with a 0.035-inch wall**. The 1/4-inch tubing is used for vent lines.

Engine installations may have different requirements, so check the engine manufacturer’s specifications, especially for fuel-injection systems.
