## Answer

The systems discussed are **electronic fuel injection and ignition systems**, such as **SDS EFI/EI** and **EFII** for Lycoming engines. ULPower’s fuel-injected engines also require a return line.

In a conventional Cozy two-tank fuel system, builders recommend a **duplex fuel selector valve**, such as the Andair FS2020, and a return line to each tank. The valve routes unused fuel back to the tank being drawn from, avoiding the risk of overfilling the other tank.

One correction: the injection supply is pressurized, but that does not necessarily mean the **return line itself is high-pressure**. Plumbing and pressure requirements depend on the particular system; follow its manufacturer’s instructions. Not all fuel injection systems need tank return lines—for example, the Airflow Performance mechanical system discussed in the archives does not require a continuous tank return.
