## Answer

For a Cozy, **streamlined wheel pants and main-gear leg fairings are the best starting points**: both are recommended in Cozy forum presentations and offer relatively substantial potential gains without the complexity of retractable gear.

1. **Wheel pants:** Improved size and shape were estimated to add **8–12 knots** in the Cozy presentations. Smooth, well-shaped pants help keep airflow attached.
2. **Main-gear leg fairings:** Streamlining the struts was estimated to add **3–5 knots**. Wider fairings can also provide room for brake lines.
3. **Cowling and cooling-air refinement:** Smoother contours, radiused inlets and a gradual cowl closeout can reduce flow separation. The presentations suggest **0–15 knots potential**, but the outcome depends heavily on the existing installation. Preserve adequate cooling and exhaust clearances.
4. **Winglet-intersection fairings:** Properly designed blends can reduce interference drag, with an estimated **1–4 knots** potential. Poorly shaped fillets can add drag instead.
5. **Nose-wheel doors and sealing air leaks:** These may offer smaller gains and can be worth addressing during other work.

The quoted speeds are presentation estimates, **not guaranteed or necessarily additive**. Tuft or oil-flow visualization can identify separation before a modification and help verify whether it worked afterward.

**Vortex generators are not a first choice for cruise drag reduction.** A specified Cozy installation was reported to lower landing speed by 7–10 knots but also reduce top speed by 1–3 knots. Retractable gear is a major modification and was not recommended in one Cozy forum presentation.

For any modification, consult experienced Cozy builders and qualified aircraft maintenance or engineering specialists, and verify cooling, clearances and handling through a careful flight-test plan.
