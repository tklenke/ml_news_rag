## Answer

The closest replacement for **EZ-Poxy 10A/87B** is **EZ-Poxy 10A resin with 83B hardener**, if available. Endurance Technologies says 83B and 87B have negligible differences in fuel resistance; they mainly differ in working time and viscosity. Gary Hunter likewise describes 83 as equivalent to 87 apart from pot life and mixed viscosity.

If you need an alternative *outside* the EZ-Poxy line, builders have discussed **Pro-Set INF-114/INF-213**, but the supplied information does not establish that it matches EZ-Poxy’s long fuel-resistance track record, especially with ethanol-containing mogas.

For a Cozy fuel tank, confirm current availability and suitability with the manufacturer, follow the specified cure—including post-curing—and consider the intended fuels. A compatible tank sealant may provide additional protection, but it is not a substitute for a suitable structural laminating epoxy.
