2. **Install Bearings**: Place the tapered roller bearings into the wheel hub as specified by Matco guidelines. Make sure they seat correctly within the wheel.

3. **Position the Wheel**: Slide the wheel onto the axle. Before tightening anything, confirm that any spacers are correctly placed and that you have the right length to avoid interference.

4. **Hand Tightening**: Hand-tighten the axle nut as firmly as you can, ensuring it is snug but not overly tight. This initial tightening will help seat the bearings properly.

5. **Check for Play**: Rotate the wheel back and forth while tightening to eliminate any play. Once the play is out and the wheel spins freely, ensure the axle nut is still tight.

6. **Preload Adjustment**: The goal with tapered roller bearings is to achieve a slight preload. If the spacers are correctly sized, you should be able to tighten the nut just enough to remove any play, leading to a minimal preload (essentially, slightly more than zero).

7. **Securing the Axle Nut**: After reaching your desired preload:
   - Identify the nearest cotter pin hole in the axle nut. 
   - Tighten or loosen the nut to align it with the closest hole, but don’t exceed one hole in either direction.
   - Insert the cotter pin into the nut to secure it.

8. **Final Check**: Once everything is tightened and secured, ensure that the wheel spins freely without unwanted drag from the seals. You can temporarily fold back the rubber seals to check if they are in contact with the wheel, which might create unnecessary drag. 

9. **Reinstall the Seals**: After verifying the preload, return the seals to their original position. If they were folded back for adjustment, carefully ensure they are seated properly to avoid contact with the wheel during operation.
