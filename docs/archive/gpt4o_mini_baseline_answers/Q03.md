## Answer

For a Cozy amateur-build aircraft using a Saber Manufacturing prop extension, the recommended torque values for the prop extension bolts vary depending on the bolt size:

- For **1/2" bolts**, the torque should be between **40 to 50 ft-lbs**.
- For **3/8" bolts**, the torque should be between **30 to 35 ft-lbs**.
- For **7/16" bolts**, the torque should be between **35 to 45 ft-lbs**.

When installing, it's essential to ensure proper cleaning and lubrication of threads, as well as following the manufacturer's guidelines for tightening in a star pattern to ensure an even and secure fit. Always refer to the specifics provided by the propeller manufacturer for the flanged connection torque values as well.
