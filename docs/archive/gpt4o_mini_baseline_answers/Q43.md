Many builders have successfully used this method, but you need to ensure there's enough space above the engine for proper mounting.

2. **Behind the Strake**: Some builders have opted to place the oil cooler behind the wing root or strake, which can also be effective. This location keeps the cooler out of direct airflow initially, but if designed properly with air ducts, it can adequately cool the oil. Successful installations have been reported, but it often requires careful design to ensure sufficient airflow through the cooler.

3. **Under the Engine**: Another alternative some builders have considered is mounting the oil cooler directly underneath the engine. While this can minimize drag and maintain a clean cowl appearance, there are concerns about vibration and ensuring sufficient airflow. Some builders using this configuration have found it successful, so it could be viable if space allows and proper mounting techniques are utilized.

4. **Lower Cowling**: There have been reports of successful installations in the lower cowling area, with adequate cooling achieved by modifying air scoops and exits to optimize airflow through the cooler.
