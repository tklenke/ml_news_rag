## Answer

When bleeding Matco brakes, particularly for your Cozy amateur-build aircraft, here are some helpful suggestions based on common practices and experiences shared by other builders:

1. **Caliper Angle**: The angle of your Matco calipers can impact the bleeding process. A more vertical position typically makes bleeding easier by allowing air to escape more effectively. If your calipers are angled, aim for a minimum angle of around 10 degrees from vertical for optimal air removal.

2. **Bleeder Valve Position**: Ideally, the bleeder valve should be the highest point on the caliper to allow air bubbles to float upwards and exit. If your setup allows it, position the bleeder screw slightly higher than the brake line inlet for better results. 

3. **Pressure Bleeding Method**: A few builders suggested using a pressure pot or a vacuum pump to help in the bleeding process. This method can efficiently push fluid through the system and expel trapped air. If you have access to a pressure bleeder, it can significantly simplify the process.

4. **Reverse Bleeding**: Some users have noted success with reverse bleeding—pushing brake fluid from the caliper back into the master cylinder. This requires a specific setup but can be effective in dislodging stubborn air pockets.

5. **Fluid Choice**: When bleeding your brakes, ensure you are using the recommended fluids. Matco recommends Mil-H-5606, but many builders have reported success with DOT 5 fluid. Just avoid DOT 3 and DOT 4, as they can damage the seals.

6. **Clearing and Flushing**: Before bleeding, consider flushing the entire brake system to ensure there are no contaminants or old fluid that could introduce air. This can be done with the aforementioned accepted fluids.

7. **Troubleshoot Persistent Air Issues**: If you have bled the system several times and still feel spongy brakes, consider checking all your fittings and lines for leaks, as any small leak could introduce air into the system. Additionally, revisiting the positioning of brake lines and ensuring there are no high points that could trap air may help.

8. **Use Transparent Tubing**: If possible, use clear tubing during the bleeding process to visually identify any air bubbles in the fluid. This can help ensure that you've completely purged the system of air.

9. **System Well-being**: Make sure all parts such as seals are in good condition, and take your time to assemble and bleed carefully. A meticulous rebuild and thorough cleaning can prevent problems during bleeding.

By keeping these tips in mind and carefully setting up your system, you should be able to effectively bleed your Matco brakes and enhance their performance on your Cozy aircraft.
